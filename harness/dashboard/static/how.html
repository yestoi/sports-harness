# How it works This page is fixed text, read by the browser and shown as text, never as markup: nothing on it came from a database and nothing on it can carry a link or a script. ## The pipeline Eight steps, in order, run every day the harness is awake. ### record The recorder writes every tick from the odds feed and the exchange to the tape, the raw record everything else replays. ### match Each market is matched to a game and a market type, so a price can be compared to the right thing. ### price A fair price is built from the sharp books' current quotes, aged by how long ago it was last recomputed. ### decide The strategy compares the fair price to the market's price and decides whether the edge, after fees, clears its bar. ### paper order When it decides yes, a simulated order is placed. No money moves and no exchange sees it; it is scored against the tape as if it had. ### fill from the recorded tape The order is filled by replaying the recorded order book against it, never by guessing: a real trade at that price, or the book crossing it, is what fills it. ### settle Once a game ends, every open order and position on it is settled against the recorded result. ### judge Once a week, and continuously as the week goes on, the settled bets are judged: was the price good, not just the outcome. ## The three jobs One person has three questions, and one surface answers each. ### is it alive and honest Pulse. Is the machine running, and is every number on this page one it can actually stand behind. ### what is it doing right now Floor. What is the executor doing this minute, well enough to see "running but wrong" as clearly as "running well". ### is the strategy any good Study. The weekly and week-to-date read, with every estimate carrying its interval and its game count. Gate is the decisive read of that same question: the twelve tests a strategy must pass before live trading is even considered, evaluated the same way every time. Ticket is the fun money: a $50-a-week parlay habit, placed by hand, that lives beside all of this without touching it. ## What paper means Every order, fill and profit figure on Pulse, Floor and Study is simulated against a recorded order book. Nothing was sent to an exchange and no money was risked. It exists so the strategy can be watched for months before anyone decides whether to trade it for real, and that decision is not made by this software. ## The honesty rules A handful of rules hold on every surface, so a reader never has to guess how much to trust a number. ### no estimate without its interval and its game count A number is never shown alone: its honest range and how many separate games it rests on travel with it, every time. ### below ten games nothing is claimed An estimate resting on fewer than ten separate games is shown grey and unstated, not rounded off and presented as if it were solid. ### stored definitions beside stored results The exact wording a test or a criterion was defined by is shown beside the number it produced, so the two can never quietly drift apart. ### every threshold imported from the code that enforces it A number that colours anything on this page is read from the same constant the system itself uses to decide, never retyped by hand in a template. ### nothing on any surface acts Every one of these pages only shows what already happened. No chart, no table and no button on them changes a strategy, a variant, a threshold or a criterion, or moves a single dollar. ## The fun money and the research never mix Ticket's $50 a week is the owner's own money, placed by hand at a real sportsbook and confirmed by hand afterward. It is not a variant, it is not scored for edge, and it is never added to or subtracted from anything the research reports. The fun money and the research never mix, on this page or anywhere else in this system. ## Source The visual design for these pages comes from one design canvas, kept for reference: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/78f3e81e-bae8-4d69-aa30-2b6697e7dd3b ## Glossary Every technical name shown anywhere on these pages, listed below.
