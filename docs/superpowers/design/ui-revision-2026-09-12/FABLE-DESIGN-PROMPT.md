# Prompt for the Fable design session

Design the next UI for my self-hosted sports harness. Read README.md, PRODUCT-BRIEF.md, EXPERIENCE-CONTRACTS.md, DATA-AND-INTEGRATIONS.md and AFTER-FABLE.md in this package before proposing the final design.

I want a game-day companion and a personal sports hub, with the personal part centered on fun parlays. I use a phone and laptop on my home network; remote access is not part of release one. The final direction is dense and dark, with selective sports-broadcast typography. I disliked the first concept's obvious generated-dashboard look: oversized greetings, rounded cards, soft gradients and excessive empty space. Use useful information and strong hierarchy to give the product character. You own the final design; the attached HTML and screenshots are references, not approved final screens.

At launch, cover NFL and college football, including player props and same-game parlays wherever DraftKings offers them. DraftKings is the primary book. The system should figure out fun parlay ideas for me; I review the reasoning and place them manually in DraftKings. A reliable link that prefills the ticket would be great where feasible. Make it clear when a link only opens an event or leg. Follow the actual accepted slip afterward, including scores, player-stat progress, corrections and settlement status.

Show me what the paper system saw, did, passed on, and learned, with a game-centered story backed by records. A zero-position day should still be useful and enjoyable. Keep the paper experiment and real fun-money tickets clearly distinguished without forcing me to navigate between disconnected products to understand the same game. Show the freshest available data. Do not include TV delay or spoiler-pause controls.

The additional sports-data budget is $100/month. Existing fun-money rules include a separate $50/week ceiling and LSU/Saints anchors. Do not change those financial settings or treat the data budget as permission to subscribe to services. Read the provider research: individual prop odds do not establish an executable same-game combined quote, and live odds do not prove live player-stat coverage. Do not invent joint probabilities or automatic bet placement.

Work with me on the final design. Resolve screen hierarchy, game-day and off-day behavior, ticket recommendations, DraftKings handoff/return, data freshness and error states. Give specific attention to dense mobile usability and readable tables. Prefer a coherent recommendation over a large menu of superficial visual themes.

Finish with the deliverables in AFTER-FABLE.md: a final design specification, responsive screens, component/interaction definitions, state examples, a data-requirement mapping, decisions/remaining limitations, and an implementation handoff that another agent can use without reconstructing this conversation. Mark fictional design data clearly. Name every deliberate change to old dashboard rules that the implementation planner must adopt.

This is a design session. Do not launch an autopilot loop, buy APIs, send messages to vendors, alter trading rules, deploy the UI, or treat the current prototypes as production implementation. We will prepare and start the implementation loop separately after the design session.
