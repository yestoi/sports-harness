How this system works
A research harness that records sports betting prices, compares Kalshi’s prices with the sharp sportsbooks’ fair prices, places simulated bets, and grades itself honestly every week. Nothing here trades for real. The point of the first three weeks is a dataset and an honest answer to one question: does the strategy get better prices than the market’s close?

Eight steps, every few minutes recorder · matcher · pricing · strategy · executor · settlement · report

01Record
Every few minutes the harness saves sportsbook odds from The Odds API and every change to Kalshi’s order book, raw, nothing thrown away.
02Match
It works out which Kalshi market is which game and which side, using team names and aliases.
03Price
From the sharp books’ odds it computes a fair probability for each market, and notes how old that price is.
04Decide
Each strategy variant compares Kalshi’s price with the fair price. A big enough gap is a candidate; every no is recorded with its reason.
05Simulated order
Candidates become simulated orders resting on the recorded order book at the price the strategy wants. No order ever reaches Kalshi.
06Fill from the tape
An order fills only when a real trade printed at our price after everyone ahead of us in line was served.
07Settle
Games end. Scores from ESPN, cross-checked against Kalshi, pay the simulated positions.
08Judge
Every week: were our prices better than the closing price, by how much, on how many games, and does it pass the twelve tests?

Words you will see glossary.json · tap any dotted term on a page

Simulated, no money at riskpaperEvery order and dollar on these pages is a simulation over recorded data. Going live is a separate decision by the operator, outside this system.
Better than the closing price?CLV, closing line valueThe gap between the price we got and the market’s final price before kickoff. Beating the close consistently is the best early evidence of real edge.
Honest rangecluster-robust 90 % intervalWhere the true number probably lies given how few games we have seen. Wide range, weak claim.
Games, not betsn_g · n_clustersBets in the same game win or lose together, so we count games. Fewer than 10 is greyed out; fewer than 30 is flagged.
Orders ahead of oursqueue_remainingHow many other people’s orders sit at our price before ours. Ours fills only after they are served.
Filled from the recorded order bookqueue_modelA simulated order fills only when a real trade printed at our price after everyone ahead of us in line was served. No guessing, no assuming we were first.
Passed onskip, with a reasonThe strategy wanted to bet but the executor declined: price gone, too close to kickoff, untrustworthy book, bankroll cap. Every pass is recorded.
The stop buttonkill switchCancels every open simulated order and places no new ones until switched off. Shown, never operated, from these pages.
Still being countedprovisionalThis week’s numbers are recomputed hourly from the same code as the final report and will change until Monday.

Rules these pages follow

No number is shown without how many games it rests on and its honest range.
Every sentence is written from the stored numbers by fixed rules, never by a model guessing.
Nothing on any page can place, cancel or change a bet. The pages only display.
The tests for going live were written before any data existed and cannot be changed from here.
