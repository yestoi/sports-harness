What this page can and cannot do

Every number on this page is simulated. No order has ever been sent to Kalshi and no money is at risk.
Live trading is a separate legal decision by the operator, taken outside this system.
The loop prepares the report and the numbers. It never decides.
The twelve tests and their thresholds were written down before any data existed and cannot be changed by this system. This page shows them; it never recomputes them.
