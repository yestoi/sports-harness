## 0. Amendments to the v2 spec

1. **Staleness (§6.2), stands under U1.** `staleness_s` stays "pricing time minus the newest sharp `last_update`"; the gate's "median feed staleness < 90 s" is evaluated on that quantity, unchanged (U1: the Odds API moves to the 5M-credit tier before 2026-09-12 and alternates are fetched every 120 s for every event inside 36 h of kickoff; featured cadence unchanged). A fair value additionally records `feed_kind` in {featured, alternate} (the endpoint that produced the newest group line), `feed_lag_s` (pricing time minus that line's `fetched_at`) and `stale_allowance_s` = the feed's configured fetch interval for that line plus the tick budget (featured: 120 + 100 = 220 s; alternates: `odds_alt_interval_near_s` or `odds_alt_interval_far_s` plus 100 s, so 220 s once the tier flips and 1000 s for rows priced before it). The `not_stale` label is `staleness_s <= max(variant.stale_s, stale_allowance_s)`. Variant configs and ids are unchanged. Rows priced before the tier flip carry their own allowance, so a report can split on it.
2. **Per-day caps (§6.5).** Exposure state is owned by the executor, rebuilt each loop from open paper orders and unsettled positions; the daily cap counts fills since 00:00 America/Chicago. Replay with `--execute` uses the same rebuild. The tick's `run_strategy` keeps labelling caps against a fresh state (it never places); the `constrained` variant's sizing realism comes from the executor.
3. **Expiry (§9.2), R8 replaces the 45 s shape.** A paper order is placed once with `expiry = kickoff - 10 min` (`exec_kickoff_cutoff_min`) and is never renewed; `Renew` does not exist. The watcher's cancels are the fast path; expiry is the guarantee. The orphan window is stated honestly: a dead process leaves paper orders resting until kickoff - 10 min (from phase 4 on, live orders too, bounded by the canary sizes). Phase 4's adapter uses the same shape plus the cancel-all fast path and never exposes `amend(expiry=...)` (the V2 amend body has no `expiration_time`). Cost if wrong: from phase 4, orphaned live orders rest longer than one cycle.
4. **Weekly report (§7.2).** Phase 3 produces tables 1-6 and 8 plus 4b (key numbers, F57); tables 7, 9, 10 print "not collected". No Claude call in phase 3. Report week N is ISO week N (R7).
5. **Benchmarks (§5.5).** `novig_mid_t5` is renamed `novig_devig_t5`: the two Odds API `novig` prices normalised to sum to 1, no power devig, main lines only (F42). `kalshi_mid_t5` and `kalshi_last_trade_pre_kick` are labelled self-convergence benchmarks. `result` and `opening_first_seen` are reported, never gated.
6. **Storage (new; U3, R17, F19).** Measured 2026-09-07: 19M `orderbook_events` rows and 8.7 GB in 4 h (458 bytes/row with indexes), 4M rows/h at peak, everything else ~1.4 GB/day. Projection: ~14 GB per game day, ~60 GB/week, ~780 GB of tape and ~960 GB total over the season, ±40 %. The harness's ceiling is 2 TB of the 3.5 TB free on `/volume1` (11 TB volume); `db_budget_gb = 2000`, dashboard red at 80 %. `orderbook_events` and `venue_trades` are range-partitioned weekly on `ts` from phase 3 (Task 2b, metadata-only `ATTACH PARTITION` of the live tables, pre-authorised by U3, run by the controller outside a game window), which also bounds the anti-wraparound vacuum to sealed partitions. Archiving or dropping sealed partitions happens only on the user's later explicit yes: the loop may propose (dashboard days-to-ceiling from the trailing 7-day growth), never execute. Postgres is tuned per R17 (§6). Kalshi documents dollar amounts to 6 decimals; every published grid bottoms at $0.0001, so `Numeric(6,4)` stays exact and no migration is scheduled (F79).
7. **Gate criteria (§9.5): exactly four deviations remain under U1 (R22, F13).** (a) "mean CLV ≥ 0 under every benchmark" excludes `result` and `opening_first_seen` (reported with CIs). (b) "≥ 150 paper fills confirmed by prints" is counted as fill events: orders with ≥ 1 `queue_model` fill, distinct games, both sports, of which ≥ 80 % on `book_source = ws` with `dirty_minutes = 0` (R12); `has_print` is a sanity assertion, not the criterion (F12). (c) "filled minus unfilled not significantly negative" becomes an equivalence bound: the 90 % cluster-robust upper bound of mean(unfilled CLV - filled CLV) < 1 pt with ≥ 20 game clusters per side, else `insufficient` (fails). (d) "zero mismatched-market incidents" is measured as orders whose market's `venue_markets.match_key` changed after placement. Nothing else deviates: "median feed staleness < 90 s" is the median of `fair_values.staleness_s` (pricing time minus the newest sharp `last_update`) over the variant's candidate signals, the U1 quantity unchanged; "zero settlement mismatches" stands, with the tightening that ≥ 90 % of settled markets carrying the variant's fills must have a `source = venue` row (R11, so the check cannot pass vacuously); every t is cluster-robust by game with G - 1 degrees of freedom; `stale = true` benchmark rows are excluded from every criterion and their share printed; every criterion counts the variant's own non-replay fills, each exec variant simulated as the sole participant. Definitions are stored with the report (§4); the gate variant is stated in §0.11 and §4.
8. **Settlement is a second, restricted writer of `orders` (F37).** §1's "only writer" claim is narrowed: the settlement job issues exactly `update orders set status = 'settled' where id = :id and status in ('filled', 'partially_filled')` and never touches open, cancelled or expired orders; it writes `ledger` settlement rows.
9. **Fee rounding (§6.0, §5.2; F11).** Kalshi ceils the trade fee to the centicent ($0.0001) per fill with a per-order rounding accumulator and whole-cent rebate (docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/fee_rounding, fetched 2026-09-07), so many small fills cost about one equivalent fill. `fee_for_order` ceils to $0.0001; per-fill fee = `ceil_to_centicent(rate x contracts x p x (1 - p))`; the 100-contract reference in `run.py` is exact at every size (roadmap phase 6 item 3 closes; the phase 2 review's "~0.6 points" was wrong).
10. **Adverse selection stays at the seed (§6.3; F56).** `as_seed = 0.01` is frozen for the pre-registered variants through phase 4. Every signal records `as_measured` (trailing mean 30-minute `nw_fill` markout by sport x 5c price bucket x side over the last 14 days, NULL below 50 fills) without using it; any promotion is a pre-registered week-4 amendment whose trailing window ends strictly before the decision instant.
11. **NO-side signals (§6.3, §6.4; U2, F18).** Registered by dated pre-registration amendment 3 before 2026-09-16 as a new secondary `sharp_two_sided` (`sides: [yes, no]`, new id) beside the six frozen ids, after a replay of the rule over the recorded tape; `MAX_SECONDARY` becomes 6 under U2. The six existing variants read `sides` as `[yes]` by default (an optional key applied after hashing, so their ids do not change). NO pricing: `fair_no = 1 - fair_p`, `best_bid_no = 1 - best_ask`, `best_ask_no = 1 - best_bid`, the fee is symmetric; every per-order quantity is expressed in the order's own side's probability space via `side_p(p, side) = p if side == 'yes' else 1 - p`. The two-sided variant is executed and, from amendment 3 onward, is the gate variant (user decision U5; §4, §9 D1); the primary's YES-only row is reported beside it.
12. **Gap recovery is our convention (V19).** Kalshi publishes no procedure; on a `seq` gap the recorder resubscribes the sid's tickers to force fresh snapshots, and the book rules in §1 treat a gap as dirty for every ticker on that sid.
13. **Scheduled loops (§4.1).** Settlement runs as its own APScheduler job (`settle_period_s = 3600`, budget 600 s) in `app-run`, never inside the recorder tick; housekeeping rides on that job.
14. **CLV ROI form (§10).** `clv_roi_net = p_bench / (p_used + fee) - 1`, the cost-inclusive form, replaces `(1/p_used)/(1/p_benchmark) - 1`; the probability-point forms are unchanged and `clv_p_net` subtracts the per-contract maker fee.
15. **Markout anchors (§10).** Markouts run from placement and additionally from the first fill of each fill set: anchors `place`, `fill` (`queue_model`), `nw_fill` (`queue_model ∪ no_watcher`) and `cross_fill` (the `snapshot_cross` instant), with a `0m` horizon on the fill anchors so "fair at fill" is a stored row.
16. **No-veto control (§6.7).** While the veto is shadow-only the no-veto control is the primary itself (identical signals); no `no_veto` secondary is registered (R2), and H9 is measured within the primary by decision label once phase 5 records decisions.

## 1. Executor process (`harness exec`, Compose service `app-exec`)

- One loop every 15 s (`exec_period_s`), single process, sync, same image, no secrets mounted, no network client. Writer of `intents`, `orders`, `order_events`, `fills`, `ledger(kind = fill)`, `exec_heartbeat`; settlement is the only other writer of `orders` (§0.8). `Executor.step()` begins with `select pg_try_advisory_lock(hashtext('harness.exec'))` and returns empty stats when the lock is held elsewhere (`exec-once` beside `app-exec` cannot double-write). Loop metrics `last_loop_ms`, `p95_loop_ms`, `loops_skipped` go on the heartbeat.
- **Intake (F34).** Reads `signals` with `decision = candidate`, the executor's own `replay` flag (false live, true in replay), variant in `exec_variants` (default `sharp_direct`, `constrained`, plus `sharp_two_sided` once amendment 3 registers it), and no intent yet: one intent per signal. The intents the loop considers are the newest per `(variant_id, venue_market_id, side)` whose signal is newer than `exec_intent_ttl_s` (900 s) and whose game has not passed `kickoff - exec_kickoff_cutoff_min`.
- **Reconcile per (variant, venue_market, side).** Newest intent is the target. No open order: place. Open order at another price (≥ 1 pt): cancel (`reprice`) and place. Same price: hold. Newest signal for the key `rejected`: cancel (`signal_rejected`).
- **Capacity (F33).** Intents are ordered by descending `edge`; once open orders across all exec variants reach `exec_max_open_orders` (150), the rest emit `Skip(exec_capacity)`, so the ceiling truncates the least valuable candidates.
- **Place** records `fair_p_at_place`, `fair_row_id_at_place`, `fair_books_json`, `venue_bid_at_place`, `venue_ask_at_place`, `venue_mid_at_place`, `queue_ahead_at_place` (resting contracts at our price on our side), `book_source` in {ws, rest, none}, `book_age_s`, `edge_at_place`, `edge_min_at_place`, `as_at_place`, `staleness_at_place` and `stale_allowance_at_place` (copied from the signal's `market_gap_snapshots` row: pricing-time values, never recomputed at the loop clock), `feed_kind`, `match_key` (the market's `venue_markets.match_key`, §5), `config_hash = sha256(variant_id, ExecSettings, EXECUTOR_VERSION)`, `expiry`. The executor's own freshness quantity is `fair_age_s = now - fair_ts`. **Post-only (F38):** before placing or repricing, `prob >= best_ask(side)` emits `Skip(post_only_reject)` (a live post-only order there is rejected); the rate is a table 8 line.
- **Missing book (R10).** A candidate whose ticker has neither a WS snapshot nor a REST ladder is still placed with `queue_ahead_at_place = NULL`, `book_source = none`, an `order_events(kind = skipped, reason = no_book)` marker against the intent, and is excluded from fill simulation until a book first exists (then `queue_ahead_at_place` and `book_first_seen_at` are set from that book); table 6 reports the share. Subscription priority: `select_ws_tickers` subscribes first to every ticker with an open paper order or an exec-variant candidate in the last hour, then fills to the 500 cap by the kickoff window (`ws_lookahead_hours = 72`, `ws_lookback_hours = 8`).
- **Watcher, every loop, per open order.** Cancel if: kill switch (`kill_switch`); `now >= expiry` (`Expire`, status `expired`); the market is no longer confidently matched (`match_status` outside `CONFIDENT_MATCHES`, so a downgrade to `fuzzy` counts) or its `match_key` changed (`unmatched`); fair stale, `now - fair_ts > max(variant.stale_s, stale_allowance_s)` (`fair_stale`, F36; placement is skipped for the same reason); venue mid moved ≥ 2 pts against the order since placement from the live book (`venue_move`); the latest fair puts edge below `edge_min / 2` (`edge_decay`). Reprice (cancel + place) if the latest fair moved ≥ 1 pt and the signal is still a candidate. No renewal (§0.3).
- **Live book (F4, F6, F7, R9).** `BookState(ticker)` is anchored on whichever of the newest clean WS snapshot (`raw.yes_dollars_fp` / `no_dollars_fp`) and the newest REST `orderbook_snapshots` row is fresher, then advanced with deltas by `id` only (`ticker = :t and id > :cursor and ts >= :anchor_ts`, no upper bound on the live path; past-instant callers bound on `ts` and order by `(ts, id)`). A book is dirty when any `gap` row with the anchor snapshot's `sid` and a higher `id` exists regardless of ticker, or when the ticker's newest event is older than `exec_book_max_age_s` (120 s; a dead recorder must not read clean). No fill, cancel or placement decision on a dirty book; `dirty_minutes` accumulates on the order; on the first clean book after a gap `queue_remaining = min(queue_remaining, resting_at(side, prob))` and the cursor jumps to that snapshot. `exec_heartbeat.book_dirty_markets` and `ws_last_event_at` feed the dashboard (red above 120 s).
- **Tape cursors (R9).** Deltas: cursor by `id` with a lower `ts` bound, per order, persisted as `tape_cursor_event_id` with `queue_remaining` and `traded_at_price` in the same transaction as the fills. Prints: no cursor; every loop rescans `ts >= placed_at - 60 s` for the ticker and relies on the fill unique key (REST backfills that land late are then seen). Both tracks of §2 persist their own cursor and queue state (`nw_*` columns).
- **Exposure.** `StrategyState` rebuilt per variant each loop: open orders' stakes, unsettled positions' stakes, fills since 00:00 local; caps are labels in `signals`; a False cap label (the first False in `CAP_LABELS` order) emits a `CapGate` action for every exec variant, written as `order_events(kind = cap_gate)`, and blocks placement only for `apply_caps` variants. **Skips (F35)** are recorded once per `(intent_id, kind, reason)` for both kinds.
- **Heartbeat and health.** `exec_heartbeat(id = 1)` updated every loop; dashboard red above 60 s; Compose healthcheck `harness exec-health` exits non-zero when `now() - last_loop_at > 120 s`; `stop_grace_period: 60s`. Kill switch active: no placements, cancel-all with reason `kill_switch`, heartbeat still updated.

## 2. Fill simulation (`harness/execution/fills.py`, pure, side-generic)

`simulate_fills(order, state, book, prints, deltas, deadline, fee_model) -> FillResult(fills, state, cross, crossed)`. It never mutates its arguments: the caller passes the book as of the order's cursor and the function walks a `book.copy()`, returning a new state. All contract quantities are `Decimal` quantized to 0.01 (F40: Kalshi counts are fractional); our own sizing stays whole contracts (`int(stake / price_target)`). The canonical taker side of a print is `taker_outcome_side or taker_side` as stored by the recorder, never defaulted (F5); a print with no side is dropped and counted. For an order on side `s` at price `q`: `opp(s)` is the other side, `price_on_side(print, s) = yes_price if s == yes else 1 - yes_price`; a print hits us when its canonical taker side is `opp(s)`.

- **Queue model (F1).** `queue_remaining` starts at `queue_ahead_at_place`; `traded_at_price` (per-price accumulator) starts at 0. Walk `prints ∪ deltas` in `ts` order, prints after deltas at equal `ts`, ignoring events after `deadline`. A hitting print with `price_on_side < q` (swept through us): `queue_remaining = 0`, fill `min(count, remaining)`, `through = true`. A hitting print at `price_on_side == q`: `consumed = min(queue_remaining, count)`, `queue_remaining -= consumed`, fill `min(count - consumed, remaining)`, `traded_at_price += count`. A delta on side `s` at price `q` with `delta < 0`: `cancels = max(0, |delta| - traded_at_price)`, `traded_at_price = max(0, traded_at_price - |delta|)`, `queue_remaining = max(0, queue_remaining - cancels)` (only genuine cancels shorten the queue). A positive delta at `q` never changes the queue (late joiners sit behind us). Each fill: `prob = q`, `contracts`, `fee = ceil_to_centicent(rate x contracts x q x (1 - q))` with `fee_type`, `fee_multiplier`, `maker_rate` stored, `filled_at`, `simulated = true`, `fill_method`, `source_trade_id`, `taker_side`, `through`, `tape_source` (ws / rest), `has_print = true`.
- **Two tracks (F3).** The watched track runs while the order is open and stops at cancel or expiry; its fills carry `fill_method = queue_model` and drive positions, ledger, P&L and exposure. The `no_watcher` track runs the same rules from placement to the order's natural deadline `expiry` regardless of the watcher, with its own persisted state (`nw_queue_remaining`, `nw_traded_at_price`, `nw_filled_contracts`, `nw_tape_cursor_event_id`, `nw_done`); its fills carry `fill_method = no_watcher`, never touch positions, ledger, P&L or exposure, and it never runs on a dirty book. Each exec variant is simulated as if it were the only participant; fills are never pooled across variants.
- **Cross fill and worst case (F41).** After applying each delta to the maintained `BookState`, the first instant `book.best_ask(s) <= q` sets `crossed` and produces one `snapshot_cross` fill of the remaining size at `q`, persisted once per order by whichever track first observes the crossing (the fill unique key on the crossing delta id dedupes the two tracks); `worst_case_fill = crossed` (the two pessimistic bounds collapse into one). Every persisted fill carries `has_print`, `fee_type`, `fee_multiplier`, `maker_rate`, `through` and `tape_source`. `fair_cross_fill` (sharp fair crossed our price between the two fair values bracketing a cancel) is kept as a separately named flag for the report only. Neither is used for positions, P&L, CLV or the gate.
- **Sanity and validity (F12).** `has_print` (a hitting print at or through our price within `[placed_at, filled_at + 60 s]`) must read 100 % on `queue_model` fills; it is asserted, not gated. Table 6 is the validity panel in §4.

## 3. Settlement, benchmarks, markouts, CLV (`harness/settlement/`)

- **Settlement job (F32).** `Settler.run()` on its own APScheduler job and its own session, hourly (`settle_period_s`), iterates a stage registry (`STAGES` in `harness/settlement/job.py`, registered in this order: `settle`, `venue_result`, `benchmarks`, `result_benchmarks`, `gap_outcomes_drain`, `order_clv`, `markouts`, `housekeeping`) under one budget `settle_budget_s = 600` checked between games and stages, `session.begin_nested()` per game with rollback and a count on failure, each stage's `StageResult` and `budget_exhausted` in `job_runs(job, started_at, finished_at, status, budget_exhausted, notes)` (never `runs.notes`). Also `harness settle` by hand.
- **Resolution (F9).** From stored ESPN scoreboard bodies, status `final` with scores: `settlements(game_id, home_score, away_score, source = espn, settled_at)`. `resolve_market` returns the YES payout in {0, 0.5, 1}: moneyline winner 1, loser 0, tie 0.5 (NFL moneylines pay $0.50 per side on a tie, `rules_secondary`); spread for team T at k.5 pays 1 when margin > k.5; total over k.5 pays 1 when the sum > k.5. `venue_settlements(venue, ticker, source, result in {yes, no, tie}, payout, settled_at)`; a NO order's payout is `1 - payout_yes`. Positions settle at `contracts x payout_side`; `ledger` rows per fill and per settlement (paper, FIFO basis); the order status update is the restricted one of §0.8.
- **Venue result (R11, F10).** After each derived settlement the job calls `KalshiPublic.fetch_market(ticker)` (`GET /markets/{ticker}`, body stored in `raw_responses` under the newest run id) and writes `source = venue` from `result` when present, retrying next pass while it is empty; the hourly `status = settled` markets fetch (recorder hotfix) is the fallback source, read from `raw_responses` with `fetched_at > now() - 7 days`. Disagreement is a `settlement_mismatch` job note and dashboard alert. `market_lifecycle_v2` waits for phase 6.
- **Kickoff moves and stale orders (F46).** Benchmark target times use the current `Game.kickoff_utc`; a game whose kickoff moved more than 5 min after its first gap snapshot (`created_at + ttk_minutes`) gets `kickoff_moved = true` and is excluded from gate means. Non-replay orders on games more than 72 h past kickoff with no settlement are a `stale_unsettled` dashboard row and job note; never auto-resolved (the 48-hour "fair price" rule is the venue's judgement and arrives through the venue result).
- **Benchmarks.** At kickoff + 5 min for every game with a matched venue market, per shape: `pinnacle_t5` (main lines from `spreads`/`totals`/`h2h`, other rungs from the alternates pairs via `spread_pair`/`total_pair`; power devig of the two-way pair), `consensus_t5/t60/t180`, `opening_first_seen`, `kalshi_mid_t5`, `kalshi_last_trade_pre_kick`, `novig_devig_t5`, and `result` after settlement (0, 0.5 or 1). `source_ts` is the book's own `last_update` (venue rows: the quote or trade time); `stale = source_ts < target_ts - 10 min`. Computed exactly once per game (unique key), never updated except `result`. Table 8 prints the stale share per type; stale rows are excluded from gate means.
- **CLV (F42).** In the side's probability space, `p_bench_side = side_p(p_bench, side)`: `clv_mid_p = p_bench - venue_mid`, `clv_bid_p = p_bench - best_bid`, `clv_target_p = p_bench - price_target` (NULL when the variant has no target on that row; `p_used_kind` in {target, best_bid}), `clv_target_p_net = p_bench - p_used - fee_per_contract(maker, p_used)`, `clv_target_roi_net = p_bench / (p_used + fee) - 1`. `gap_outcomes` is restricted to `pinnacle_t5`, `consensus_t5`, `kalshi_mid_t5`, `result` (the whole-population H2 map), drained continuously from a `job_state` watermark on `market_gap_snapshots.id` in batches of 50 000. Per order, `order_clv(order_id, benchmark_type, p_bench, p_used, p_used_kind, clv_p, clv_p_net, clv_roi_net, stale)` is computed for every non-replay order across every benchmark type of the order's shape (the seven gate types plus `result` and `opening_first_seen`, the last two reported only) in the order's side space with `p_used = orders.prob` and `p_used_kind = order`; table 2's contrasts, the `clv` view and gate criteria 3, 6 and 7 read `order_clv`. H8 is descriptive only at the 120-900 s cadence.
- **Markouts (F15).** PK `(order_id, anchor, horizon)`, `anchor` in {place, fill, nw_fill, cross_fill} (fill = first `queue_model` fill; nw_fill = first fill in `queue_model ∪ no_watcher`; cross_fill = the `snapshot_cross` instant for crossed orders; unfilled orders get `place` only), horizons 0m (the anchor instant itself, fill anchors only, so "fair at fill" is a stored row), 1m, 5m, 30m, 120m, close (kickoff - 5 min). `markout_net(h) = fair_p(h) - p_used - fee_per_contract(maker, p_used)`, positive = we profit, `p_used` = fill price for fill anchors and `prob` for `place`. The fair is the newest direct sharp consensus with `newest_book_ts <= horizon_ts` (fallback `created_at`), recorded as `fair_row_id`, `fair_book_ts`, `fair_changed = fair_row_id != fair_row_id_at_place`, `fair_age_s`; `venue_mid` from the last `venue_quotes` row within 60 s else the WS book mid at that instant (`source` in {quote, ws_book, none}; a book older than 120 s at the instant is `none`, F36), `mid_age_s`. Fair markouts are reported only when `fair_changed`; 1 m and 5 m markouts come off the WS mid. `adverse_drift = fair_p(nw_fill, 0m) - side_p(fair_p_at_place)` on `fair_changed` rows.
- **Views.** `positions` (open contracts and basis per variant x ticker x side, `queue_model` fills of non-replay orders minus settled), `clv` (per order, each benchmark, net of fee), `order_episodes` (a reprice chain collapsed to one episode per variant x market x side).

## 4. Report, gate, dashboard, replay

- **Unit of analysis (F14).** Every cell shows `n_obs` and `n_clusters` (games); greyed below 10 clusters, flagged below 30. `cluster_ci` uses `SE² = (G/(G-1)) x Σ_g(Σ_{i in g}(x_i - x̄))² / N²`, `bound = x̄ ± t_{0.95, G-1} x SE`, with the t quantile from the regularised incomplete beta. Table 1 adds distinct markets and (market, day); table 2 is a paired difference per shared snapshot (variant - primary, clustered by game; non-executed variants report snapshot CLV at their own `price_target`); table 3 collapses reprice chains to episodes and reports markouts under `queue_model`, `queue_model ∪ no_watcher` and `queue_model ∪ snapshot_cross` (anchors `fill`, `nw_fill`, `cross_fill`; 1 m and 5 m markouts from the WS mid, fair markouts only where `fair_changed`), split by `feed_kind` and staleness at place (≤ 220 s / > 220 s); table 4 states the per-snapshot estimand and its `n_clusters`.
- **Mispricing map (R13, F16).** Grid: price {20-35, 35-50, 50-65, 65-80} x TTK {> 24 h, 3-24 h, < 3 h} x sport x market type = 72 cells per fair source, with `feed_kind` and a `staleness_s` bucket (< 120, 120-300, 300-1000, > 1000) as strata; the headline H2 claim is from `feed_kind = featured`. Panels `gap_mid` (stored as `fair - mid`, printed in the header), `gap_maker_net`, `clv_mid_p` vs `pinnacle_t5`. Shrinkage within sport x market type: `τ² = max(0, var(m_i) - mean(se_i²))` from cluster-robust `se_i²`, `B_i = τ² / (τ² + se_i²)`, `m̃_i = B_i m_i + (1 - B_i) m̄`; τ² = 0 prints "no heterogeneity detected". Families: A the 72 direct cells (BH q = 0.10 on two-sided cluster-robust t), B the 72 derived cells, C the five variant contrasts (Holm); greyed cells excluded and counted. The §9.6 criterion is judged on the posterior interval `m̃ ± t_{G-1} sqrt(B se²)`; BH-adjusted intervals on raw means are reported beside it. The week-2 report (Mon 2026-09-21 09:00 CT) writes `docs/reports/2026-w38-selected.json`; week 3 evaluates only that set on week-3 data. Table 4b: derived - direct where both exist, bucketed by distance to the nearest key number (3, 7), so H4 is stated net of the model's own error.
- **Convergence lag (F43).** Featured shapes only; sharp move time = `fair_values.newest_book_ts`, venue time = the first WS mid crossing 50 % of the move; reported as the interval `[max(0, lag - interval), lag]` with the sampling floor as a column, negative lags (venue first) and the censored share, Kaplan-Meier median. H6's NFL burst window (T-100..T-60 min at 20 s) is where sub-minute lags are visible.
- **Table 6, validity panel (F12).** `queue_ahead_at_place` distribution and the share equal to 0 or NULL; at-versus-through composition of fill-triggering prints; book coverage at placement (ws / rest / none) and dirty order-minutes; queue accuracy (REST ladder vs WS book within 60 s: median absolute difference, share within 10 %); queue-consumption ratio (contracts printed at or through our price before the first fill ÷ `queue_ahead_at_place`); fill counts under `queue_model`, `∪ no_watcher`, `∪ snapshot_cross`, `worst_case`; tape source of fill-triggering prints; realised fee per contract; `has_print` share (must be 100 %).
