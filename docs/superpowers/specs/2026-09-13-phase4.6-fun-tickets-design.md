## 1. Ticket surface (`harness/dashboard/snapshots/ticket.py`, `static/js/ticket.mjs`, `sentences.py`)

Order on both widths: **This week's ideas**, **Live tickets**, **Season** (design §2). Three drafts across at 1440 px, stacked at 390 px.

### 1.1 Ideas and the draft slip

- The builder query widens from `status in ('placed','alive','cashed','busted','void')` to include `proposed` cards of the current Chicago ISO week (`ticket.py:63`), grouped into three **slots** by shape: `smart`, `lottery` (cross-game), `lottery` same-game (`correlated = true` and every leg in one game; design §2.1). A slot holds at most one open draft; a placed card moves to Live tickets and its slot shows `placed` with a link down.
- A draft renders as the slate slip with exactly three marks (dashed perforation, `PROPOSED · NOT PLACED` badge, hollow rings) and the body-size payout line `would pay $137.50 at +450`. Contents top to bottom per design §2.1: anchor line, rationale, legs (plain text; the exact selection in small technical type with the DraftKings price and its age; the context line; a same-game relationship note), footer (combined price as `quoted` / `calculated` / `none`, hold line, week line `$25 recorded · $25 left of $50`), and three actions: **Open in DraftKings** labelled by `link_capability` (`Open selection`, `Open event`, `Copy selections`; `Open full slip` is never produced in release one, §3.3), **I placed this** (§5), **Not this one** (§5.4).
- The payload adds `ideas: {slots: [{shape, card | null, reason_code | null, next_build_at}], week_recorded, week_left}`; every card carries `policy_version`, `combined_kind`, `link_capability`, `legs[].selection` (the exact-selection string), `legs[].context` (§1.3), `legs[].dk_link`, `legs[].offered`. All strings pass `sanitize_reason` on the way into the payload as today.
- Opening a link, copying or leaving the page records nothing (design §2.1).

### 1.2 No idea: the named reasons

A slot with no draft carries one `reason_code` from the fixed vocabulary of design §2.2 (`no_anchor_priced`, `anchor_bye`, `no_props_fresh`, `player_unmatched`, `market_unsupported`, `week_at_cap`, `not_built_yet`, `builder_failed`) and `sentences.py` gains `idea_reason_phrase(code)` with the eight sentences verbatim; an unknown code renders as itself and is reported in `sentences_gaps` (design §8.2). The builder stage writes the code per slot into `job_state` key `parlay_build:<year>-<week>:<sport>:<shape>` (§2.4) so the surface never guesses why a slot is empty. The between-cards facts (next build day, anchor rule, budget left) stay under the sentence.

### 1.3 Live tickets

The existing slip with the design §2.3 additions, each from a recorded source:

- **Stat line** on a prop leg: `208 of 225 passing yards · 17 to go · ESPN 40 s ago` from the newest `player_stat_events` row for `(game_id, player_id, stat)`; a player absent from the latest update reads `unchanged · last seen 2 min ago` (the newest row's age), never zero; no row at all reads `no stat yet`.
- **Needs phrase** extended (§4.4): whole-number pushes called pushes; `reached 59 of 50 · provisional until final`; `under by 31 · live until the game ends`.
- **Correction note**: `corrected from 12 to 8 · ESPN` when the newest row has `correction = true`; no animation, no lamp replay.
- **Sharps say** only when `parlay_leg_probs` has a row for the leg; otherwise `no sharp read` and no history bar (props have none unless a sharp book prices them; the recorder's `_LEG_PROB_ROWS` stays game-line only in release one).
- **Settlement pending**: all legs `hit` and no `parlay_ledger.kind = 'return'` row reads `all legs hit · awaiting DraftKings settlement`, lamps lit, no stamp; `CASHED` lands on the return row; a `void` row reads `voided by DraftKings · stake returned` and stamps `VOID` in `--slip-muted`; `BUSTED` on the first miss as today.
- **Corrected placement**: a card with `parlay_placement_corrections` rows shows `corrected` beside the stake; tapping shows the original figures (a `<details>`).
- `#ticket/card/<card_id>` scrolls to and expands one slip (design §1).

### 1.4 Season

Layout unchanged; tile labels `staked`, `returned (includes stake)`, `net`; grey chips for `declined` and `expired` cards (`status = 'void'` with `declined_reason`), which are not in the ledger and move no figure.

### 1.5 States

The nine states of design §2.5 are fixtures in the plan (`tests/test_snap_ticket.py`) and are rendered by the walker rows in verify.md: ideas present nothing placed; one draft placed; no idea for a shape; every shape at cap; stale quote (price age over `leg_max_age_minutes`: the leg's price line in `--warn`, footer `prices older than 30 min · repriced at the next tick`, actions stay); removed prop (`offered = false`: leg reads `no longer offered by DraftKings`, the Open button drops to `Copy selections`, I placed this stays); declined; snapshot section failed (`unavailable`, never `no ticket live`); disconnected browser (the shell's staleness banner; the sheet refuses to submit and says why).

## 2. Builder, policy and the recorder's parlay sources (`harness/parlay/`, `harness/settlement/parlay_build.py`, `harness/recorder/tick.py`)

### 2.1 Policy (`harness/parlay/parlay.yaml`, `config.py`)

`parlay.yaml` gains `policy_version: "2026.09-1"` and a `props` block: `families: [pass_yds, rush_yds, rec_yds, receptions, anytime_td]`, `books: [draftkings]`, `max_prop_legs_smart: 2`, `same_game: {min_legs: 3, max_legs: 6, distinct_player_or_market: true}` (anchor plus 2 to 5 legs from the anchor's game), `disqualifiers: [player_unmatched, market_unsupported, stale_price]`, `prop_events_max: 24` (§3.2). Stakes, `weekly_budget`, `smart_stake`, `lottery_stake`, `lottery_cards_max`, `anchors`, `smart_legs`, `lottery_legs`, `leg_max_age_minutes`, `pool_window_hours`, `pool_min_edge`, `expiry_days` are untouched (F03). Every card records `policy_version`. Changing the policy is a config commit, never a UI action.

### 2.2 Build (`build.py`)

`build_card(session, settings, sport, week, kind, now, *, same_game: bool = False, parent_card_id: int | None = None)`:

- The **game-line pool** is unchanged (`_POOL`: candidate signals with a `direct` fair value and edge over `pool_min_edge`, priced at the newest DraftKings row inside `leg_max_age_minutes`).
- The **prop pool** is a second source: the newest DraftKings `odds_snapshots` row per `(game_id, player_id, stat, point, side)` inside the age limit (§3), for events whose kickoff is inside 36 h (the prop fetch window, §3.2; `pool_window_hours` bounds the signal pool, not the prop pool). A prop leg has no signal and no fair value: its `true_p` contribution is the book's implied probability with the vig removed across the two sides of the same line (the existing `_hold` arithmetic then reports a hold, never an edge). A prop leg is never labelled +EV (EXPERIENCE-CONTRACTS §1).
- **Smart card** (cross-game, 3-4 legs): anchor first as today, then legs by edge; at most `max_prop_legs_smart` prop legs, each from a different game than any other leg. **Lottery card** (6-8 legs, correlated allowed) as today plus prop legs. **Same-game lottery card** (`same_game=True`): the anchor's game only; anchor plus `same_game.min_legs-1 .. max_legs-1` legs, each a different player or market; `correlated = true`; `combined_kind = 'calculated'` with the existing correlation sentence.
- **Disqualifiers** are recorded, not silent: a candidate prop whose player is unmatched (§4.2) is dropped and counted `player_unmatched`; an unsupported family `market_unsupported`; a stale price `stale_price`. When no card of a shape can be built the stage records the dominant reason (§2.4).
- **Links**: each leg carries `dk_link` and `dk_sid` from the newest snapshot's link columns (§3.3); the card's `link_capability` is the minimum over its legs (`selection` when every leg has an outcome link, `event` when every leg has an event link, else `none`).
- `combined_kind` is `calculated` (independence product, as today) or `none` (a leg without a decimal price); `quoted` exists in the vocabulary for a future combined-quote source and is never written in release one (D8).
- Rationale as today (`write_rationale`, template or the bounded model call, sanitized); for a same-game card the relationship notes come from a fixed table keyed by the pair of stats (`pass_yds`+`rec_yds`: "both move on a <QB> completion"; `pass_yds`+`anytime_td`: "a touchdown pass moves only the yards"; …), never from the model.

### 2.3 Reprice, offered, expiry (recorder tick source `_parlay_reprice`)

Every 900 s tick outside game windows (the weather source's cadence rule, `ALLOWED_CADENCES`), for every `proposed` card of the current week: each leg is repriced at the newest DraftKings row for its exact selection (`dk_american`, `dk_decimal`, `odds_snapshot_id`, `dk_link`, `dk_sid`; the leg's line is **not** changed: a moved line leaves the price stale and the sheet's `LineMoved` path handles it); a leg whose selection has no row inside `leg_max_age_minutes` × 4 is marked `offered = false`; `dk_payout_est`, `hold_est`, `dk_combined_at` are recomputed. A **placed** card is never touched (EXPERIENCE-CONTRACTS §1: a refresh must not alter a recorded ticket). Bounded: at most `3 × lottery_cards_max + 1` cards; never fails a tick (the `_leg_probs` pattern).

`expire_cards` (today CLI only) runs in the builder stage: a `proposed` card older than `expiry_days` becomes `void`, `declined_reason = 'expired'`.

### 2.4 The builder stage (`harness/settlement/parlay_build.py`)

A new settle-job stage `parlay_build`, registered after `parlay_grade` (the stage list in `job.py`; every stage idempotent). Each run: (1) expire; (2) for each `(sport, shape)` slot of the current Chicago week whose build time has passed (`ncaaf` Friday 18:00 CT, `nfl` Saturday 18:00 CT, the existing between-cards rule) and which has no `proposed` or placed card, `build_card` once, recording the outcome in `job_state` `parlay_build:<year>-<week>:<sport>:<shape>` as `{built: card_id}` or `{reason: <code>, at}`; (3) for a card declined since the last run (`status = 'void'`, `declined_reason = 'declined'`, no child), build one replacement of the same shape with `parent_card_id`, once (a declined replacement is not replaced again: the slot shows the no-idea sentence). A build failure is caught, logged and recorded as `builder_failed`. Budget: the stage records `elapsed_s` like every other stage (fix 47) and stops after 20 s.

Cards of a slot whose week is at cap are not built (`week_at_cap`): `week_staked` from `placement.py`.

## 3. Props and links from the Odds API (`harness/feeds/odds_api.py`, `harness/normalize/odds.py`, `harness/recorder/tick.py`)

### 3.1 Request

`fetch_event_props(sport, event_id)` calls the per-event endpoint the recorder already uses for alternates (`/sports/{sport}/events/{event_id}/odds`) with `markets = PROP_MARKETS` (`player_pass_yds,player_rush_yds,player_reception_yds,player_receptions,player_anytime_td` plus the `_alternate` variants of the first four; the venue's catalogue names these keys and says player props are served one event at a time), `includeLinks=true`, `includeSids=true`, `bookmakers` unchanged (gate 5). Cost is `unique markets returned × regions` per call (the venue's guide; one region); the client records `x-requests-last` per call as today.

### 3.2 Watched set and allocation (pre-loaded 3)

- The **watched prop events** per tick: events of `nfl` and `ncaaf` kicking off inside 36 h that have an anchor team or a `signals` row with a direct fair inside `pool_window_hours`, ordered by kickoff, at most `prop_events_max` (24) per sport. Cadence 900 s (the old alternates cadence), never on the 120 s game-window tick and never in quiet hours, inside a sub-budget of 15 s of the tick (the alternates pattern, `ALTERNATES_BUDGET_S`).
- **Allocation**: `Settings.odds_prop_monthly_credits = 300_000` (6 % of the 5M tier). The recorder sums `x-requests-last` of prop calls per Chicago month in `source_state` key `odds_props:<YYYY-MM>` (`last_fetched_at` plus a new nullable `credits_used` column, additive) and goes **dormant** for the month when the sum reaches the allocation (`ctx["props"] = {"skipped": "budget"}`, a `prop_skipped_budget` counter, a Pulse WATCH rule `prop_budget`). **The strategy feed is protected first**: props are also skipped whenever `x-requests-remaining < CREDITS_WATCH_FRACTION × odds_monthly_credits` (40 %), before the 20 % gate-5 line is near. Both caps are numbers in config, enforced in code, dormant when exceeded (Conformance 6).
- Measured expectation, for the verify row: ≤ 48 events × ≤ 9 markets ≈ 430 credits per tick, 4 ticks an hour inside the 36 h window; a full NFL-plus-college week ≈ 70k credits, a month ≈ 300k. The row records the actual per-day prop credits.

### 3.3 Normalizer and storage

`parse_odds_body` keeps `description` (the player name for prop outcomes), `link` and `sid` per outcome and per event (`includeLinks`/`includeSids`), and `market_type` carries the venue key. `odds_snapshots` gains additive columns `player_name String(80) null`, `player_id int null` (resolved through §4.2 at normalize time when the game's roster is known; null otherwise), `link String(300) null`, `sid String(64) null`. `upsert_odds_rows` resolves a prop outcome's side from `name` (`Over`/`Under`/`Yes`/`No`) and keeps the point as the line; the team resolver is not called for prop outcomes. The forbidden-table list is unchanged: the Ticket builder never reads `odds_snapshots`; the builder stage (a job) does, bounded by `ix_odds_game_type_fetched`.

`full_slip` does not exist in the product (FEASIBILITY 3, the venue's release note: per-outcome and per-event links only). Release one produces `selection`, `event` or `none`; a label above `event` needs the owner's device verification (pre-loaded 3) and is not planned.

## 4. Player stats and identity (`harness/feeds/espn.py`, `harness/normalize/players.py`, `harness/parlay/needs.py`)

### 4.1 Source (pre-loaded 2, FEASIBILITY 1)

ESPN's summary endpoint on the recorder's host: `GET {espn_base_url}/{nfl|college-football}/summary?event={espn_event_id}` (`games.espn_event_id` exists). `boxscore.players[team].statistics[category].athletes[]` carries `passing` (YDS, TD), `rushing` (CAR, YDS, TD), `receiving` (REC, YDS, TD, TGTS) with ESPN athlete ids; `scoringPlays` carries the scorers. Rosters: `GET {espn_base_url}/{sport}/teams/{team_id}/roster` on the same host, once per team per week, for the teams of the watched prop events (§3.2). No paid provider in release one; the $100 ceiling stays in reserve; the first live NFL window measured (§11 row 4) decides whether a paid feed is needed, which is gate 7 and the user's account action.

### 4.2 Identity (`players`, `player_unmatched`)

New table `players(id serial PK, sport, espn_id String(16), name String(80), team_id int, position String(6), updated_at)` with a unique index on `(sport, espn_id)`. Filled from the roster fetch. A prop outcome's `description` is matched to a player of the game's two teams by a normalized name (lower-case, diacritics folded, punctuation and suffixes `Jr.`, `Sr.`, `II`, `III`, `IV` removed, an initial matching a first name): exactly one candidate matches or the outcome is `player_unmatched` (counted per tick in `ctx["props"]`; the leg is never built). Ambiguity never picks; the box score's own ids are the live key.

### 4.3 Collector (recorder tick source `_player_stats`)

For every in-progress game with a prop leg on a `placed` or `alive` card (bounded: the cards of one week), on every game-window tick (120 s): one summary fetch per game inside a 10 s sub-budget, at most 8 games per tick; then one fetch at `final` for grading. Rows are written to `player_stat_events(id bigserial, game_id, player_id, ts, source_ts null, stat String(12), value Numeric(8,2), source String(12) 'espn', raw_id bigint, correction bool default false)` only when the value **changed** (the `game_score_events` rule); a value lower than the previous row's is `correction = true`. `anytime_td` is derived as `rushing TD + receiving TD ≥ 1` per `market_def` ("scores a rushing or receiving TD"; passing TDs excluded). A player missing from an update writes nothing (the surface reads the age). Never fails a tick.

### 4.4 Needs and grading (`needs.py`, `parlay_grade.py`)

`LegSpec` gains `stat`, `period`, `operator`, `player_id`; `StatState(stat, value, source_ts, final: bool)` is a second input beside `ScoreState`. `needs(leg, score, stat=None)` for a prop: `over`/`atleast`: `value < line` → `<line-value> to go`; `value >= line` before final → `reached <value> of <line> · provisional until final`; `under`: `under by <line-value> · live until the game ends` or `over by <value-line>`; `yes`: `no touchdown yet` / `scored`. `leg_outcome` for a prop returns `hit`/`miss` only when the game is final (`over`, `atleast`, `under`, `yes` per the operator; a whole-number `over`/`under` line equal to the value is `push`), `None` before; a missing `StatState` at final is `None` and `parlay_grade` leaves the leg pending and logs `stat_missing` (never a miss by absence). Period props are out of release one (`period = 'game'` only; D3). Pushes and voids follow the existing rules; a void leg on a same-game card uses the recorded book outcome (the owner records a `void` through the correction route, §5.3), never a recomputed price.

## 5. Placement, corrections and decline (`harness/dashboard/app.py`, `harness/parlay/placement.py`)

### 5.1 `POST /api/parlay/placed`

Body `{card_id, confirmation_id (UUID v4), stake, accepted_odds (American int), leg_lines: {seq: line} | null, note | null}`. The route (LAN listener only, session required, §6) opens one transaction, takes `select ... for update` on the card, and:

1. If a placement with this `confirmation_id` exists → `200` with that placement (idempotent).
2. Else calls `mark_placed(session, card_id, accepted_odds, stake, now, leg_lines)`, which keeps every refusal: `CardNotPlaceable` (a different id on an already placed card included) → `409 {"refusal": "card_not_placeable"}`; `BudgetExceeded` → `409 {"refusal": "budget_exceeded", "recorded", "left"}`; `LineMoved` → `409 {"refusal": "line_moved", "moved": {seq: [was, now]}}`.
3. Records `confirmation_id` and `note` on the placement; the cap check runs inside the same transaction as the ledger insert (design §3.3).

`mark_placed` gains `confirmation_id: str | None = None` and `note: str | None = None` (additive keywords; the CLI passes none). The weekly cap is never relaxed by the UI (design §3.2); an out-of-budget placed ticket cannot be recorded and the sheet says so; remediation is a policy decision for the user, not a route.

### 5.2 `POST /api/parlay/correct`

Body `{card_id, field, new_value, note | null}`, `field ∈ {stake, accepted_odds, leg_line:<seq>, status}`. For a **placed or alive** card it writes one `parlay_placement_corrections(id, card_id, ts, field String(16), old_value String(32), new_value String(32), note String(200))` row per call and applies the change: `stake` also writes an offsetting `parlay_ledger` `stake` row (the delta, signed) in the same transaction so the cap and the season figures follow; `accepted_odds` recomputes `dk_payout_actual`; `leg_line` sets the leg's threshold. Corrections after grading (`card.status in ('cashed','busted')` or any leg graded) → `409 {"refusal": "graded"}`; the cap is re-checked on a stake increase (`budget_exceeded`). Grading uses the corrected terms. A `status` correction to `void` on a placed card records DraftKings' void (`parlay_ledger.kind = 'void'`, stake back) with the note; it is the one status value the route accepts on a placed card.

### 5.3 Idempotency and concurrency

Two submits with one `confirmation_id` record one placement and one ledger row; two ids on one card record one placement and one `409`; a phone and a laptop submitting together serialize on the row lock (the second sees the first's placement). Corrections carry no confirmation id: the same field corrected twice with the same value writes two rows (an audit trail), which the surface shows as one `corrected` mark with the newest values.

### 5.4 Decline: `Not this one` (D5)

The design's decline action is carried by `POST /api/parlay/correct` with `field = "status"`, `new_value = "declined"` on a **proposed** card: the card becomes `void`, `declined_reason = 'declined'`, one corrections row `(status, proposed, void, note)` records it, no money moves, and the builder stage builds a replacement on its next run (§2.4; the slot reads `replacement is built within the hour`). No third route exists (U9 names two).

### 5.5 CSRF and body rules

Both routes require the session cookie (`SameSite=Strict`), `Content-Type: application/json`, the header `X-Requested-With: sports-ui`, and an `Origin` equal to the LAN listener's own origin; anything else is `403`. Bodies are size-limited (4 KiB), every string sanitized (`sanitize_reason`), `note` truncated at 200. Errors carry a refusal code, never a stack trace.

### 7.1 Board card (design §4.4)

### 7.2 Detail payload (`floor.details[game_id]`)

Built by the Floor builder for games in the board window only, keyed by game id, each payload capped at 24 KiB and the set at 20 games (the board's size), from the bounded tables only (the five forbidden tables stay forbidden):

1. **Scoreline**: `games` and the newest `game_score_events` row (period, clock, `source age`); before kickoff the kickoff in Central and the countdown. The clock is the source's state, never animated.
2. **Our position**: `orders` + `fills` + `ledger` for the game's `venue_markets` (the `_EXPOSURE` shape): contracts, entry price, paper stake, and the result the position needs in the slip's phrasing; before kickoff "what the strategy is watching on this game and until when" (the primary's open intents and orders and their expiry); during play `placed before kickoff · following the outcome`; never a word implying in-play trading. No position: `no position on this game` with the reason from the story.
3. **Decision story**, chronological, each row with its own time and unit, from: `signals` bounded by the game's `venue_market_id`s through `ix_signal_market_created` inside the board window, **summarized** to one `evaluated` row per market and side (`N evaluations from t1 to t2 · last: fair 62¢ against 58¢ · <reason phrase>`; the per-tick rows are too many to list and are not the story), `intents` (skips), `orders` and `order_watch_samples` (resting, queue ahead), `fills` (`(simulated)` always), `order_events` (cancelled/expired with the reason phrase), `gap_outcomes` (closing benchmark), `ledger` (settlement in paper dollars), and derived `gap` rows for any span over 30 minutes between two rows before kickoff. `evaluated and passed`, `not evaluated` and `evaluation failed` (a `runs.status = degraded` tick covering the market) are three different first rows. A row expands (`<details>`) to its ids, variant and raw figures.
4. **Markets**: the game's priced markets (`fair_values` newest per market through `ix_fair_game_type_created`, the open orders' book figures Floor already computes): market, fair, book, edge now, age.
5. **On your ticket**: one line linking to `#ticket/card/<id>` when a `placed`/`alive` card has a leg on this game; no amount, no leg detail (F02; the only crossing, one-way).

Five additive indexes for the story, built the fix 45 way (`CONCURRENTLY`, declared on the models, revision §9): `ix_intents_market_created (venue_market_id, created_at)`, `ix_order_events_order_ts (order_id, ts)`, `ix_fills_order_ts (order_id, ts)`, `ix_gap_outcomes_order (order_id)`, `ix_ledger_order (order_id)`. The builder's bounded-query test (2000 ms statement timeout, no forbidden table) covers the detail; `serve.snapshot_ms` and `FLOOR_P95_BUDGET_MS` judge it (design §4.3); the scheduler self-guard degrades a slow detail rather than harming the loop.

### 7.3 Rendering

`#floor/game/<id>` opens the detail: at 1440 px a full-width card under the board row containing the game with a close control; at 390 px a full-height sheet over the board; Escape and the back button close it; one open at a time; the scoreline in the display face at 40 px with the score in the numeral face (broadcast place 2 of 3).
