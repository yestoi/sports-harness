### 1.1 Ideas and the draft slip

## 3. Props and links from the Odds API (`harness/feeds/odds_api.py`, `harness/normalize/odds.py`, `harness/recorder/tick.py`)

### 3.1 Request

`fetch_event_props(sport, event_id)` calls the per-event endpoint the recorder already uses for alternates (`/sports/{sport}/events/{event_id}/odds`) with `markets = PROP_MARKETS` (`player_pass_yds,player_rush_yds,player_reception_yds,player_receptions,player_anytime_td` plus the `_alternate` variants of the first four: nine market keys), `includeLinks=true`, `includeSids=true`, `bookmakers` unchanged (gate 5). Cost is `unique markets returned × regions` per call (the venue's guide; one region): at most 9 credits per call. The client records `x-requests-last` per call as today.

### 3.2 Watched set, cadence and allocation (pre-loaded 3), derived

- **Watched prop events** per sport: events kicking off inside `prop_window_hours` (24 h) that have an anchor team or a `signals` row with a direct fair inside `pool_window_hours`, ordered anchor first then by kickoff, at most `prop_events_max` (16) per sport. **Calls per tick** at most `prop_calls_per_tick` (16) across both sports, oldest-fetched first (a rotation), so 32 events refresh inside two ticks (30 min ≤ `leg_max_age_minutes`). Cadence: only on the 900 s tick (never the 120 s game-window tick, never quiet hours), inside a 40 s sub-budget of the tick (the `ALTERNATES_BUDGET_S` pattern; the existing alternates cadence is 120 s and is not what this reuses). A call that fails counts against the budget and is retried on the next tick.
- **Arithmetic**: ≤ 16 calls × ≤ 9 credits = 144 credits per tick; 4 ticks an hour = 576 an hour; a populated 24 h window ≈ 14k; a week has two populated sport windows (Saturday college, Sunday NFL) plus Thursday/Friday/Monday games ≈ 40k; a month ≈ 170k. The verify row records the actual prop credits per day.
- **Allocation**: `Settings.odds_prop_monthly_credits = 300_000` (6 % of the 5M tier; ~1.75× the derived need). The recorder sums `x-requests-last` of prop calls per Chicago month in `source_state` key `odds_props:<YYYY-MM>` (`last_fetched_at` plus a new nullable `credits_used` column, additive) and goes **dormant** for the month when the sum reaches the allocation (`ctx["props"] = {"skipped": "budget"}`, a `prop_skipped_budget` counter, a Pulse WATCH rule `prop_budget`). **The strategy feed is protected first**: props are also skipped whenever `x-requests-remaining < CREDITS_WATCH_FRACTION × odds_monthly_credits` (40 %), before the 20 % gate-5 line is near. Both caps are numbers in config, enforced in code, dormant when exceeded (Conformance 6). The verify.md credits band ("Odds credits per day … cadence change or key leak") gains an interpretation line: prop credits are reported separately and the band applies to the strategy feed's share.
- **College coverage effect, stated**: with 16 events per sport and the signal-or-anchor rule, college props cover LSU's game and the matched-and-priced games of the slate, not every college game; a verify row counts college events watched against college events played that week, and a college family DraftKings offers that the set never fetched is reported as an unmet requirement (pre-loaded 1), never narrowed silently.

### 3.3 Normalizer, keys, links and storage

- `parse_odds_body` keeps `description` (the player name for prop outcomes), `link` and `sid` per outcome and per event. ### 4.2 Identity (`players`, `player_unmatched`)

New table `players(id serial PK, sport, espn_id String(16), name String(80), team_id int, position String(6), updated_at)` with a unique index on `(sport, espn_id)`. Filled from the roster fetch. A prop outcome's `description` is matched to a player of the game's two teams by a normalized name (lower-case, diacritics folded, punctuation and suffixes `Jr.`, `Sr.`, `II`, `III`, `IV` removed, an initial matching a first name): exactly one candidate matches or the outcome is `player_unmatched` (counted per tick in `ctx["props"]`; the leg is never built). Ambiguity never picks; the box score's own ids are the live key.

### 4.3 Collector (recorder tick source `_player_stats`)

For every in-progress game with a prop leg on a `placed` or `alive` card (bounded: the cards of one week), on every game-window tick (120 s): one summary fetch per game inside a 10 s sub-budget, at most 8 games per tick; after `final`, one more fetch per tick until a summary with the final box score is read (at most 6 h; a card left pending after that is the hung-leg state). Rows are written to `player_stat_events(id bigserial, game_id, player_id, ts, source_ts null, stat String(12), value Numeric(8,2), source String(12) 'espn', raw_id bigint, correction bool default false)` **for carded players only**, only when the value **changed** (the `game_score_events` rule). ESPN's summary carries no per-stat timestamp, and sequential polls of one endpoint return the source's current state, so **a later poll reporting a lower value is a correction** (`correction = true`); `source_ts` is null for this source and the surface shows the fetch age. `anytime_td` is derived from `scoringPlays` (any touchdown whose scorer is the player: rushing, receiving, return or recovery; passing excluded) per the recorded `market_defs.anytime_td`; when the play type cannot be decided the leg stays pending (never a miss). A player missing from an update writes nothing (the surface reads the age). Never fails a tick.

### 4.4 Needs and grading (`needs.py`, `parlay_grade.py`)

`LegSpec` gains `stat`, `period`, `operator`, `player_id`, all defaulting to `None`/`'game'` so the three existing construction sites are unchanged; `StatState(stat, value, source_ts, final: bool)` is a second input beside `ScoreState`. `needs(leg, score, stat=None)` for a prop: `over`/`atleast`: `value < line` → `<line-value> to go`; `value >= line` before final → `reached <value> of <line> · provisional until final`; `under`: `under by <line-value> · live until the game ends` or `over by <value-line>`; `yes`: `no touchdown yet` / `scored · provisional until final`. **Final grade per operator** (`leg_outcome`, only when the game is final and a `StatState` with `final = True` exists): `over`: `value > line` hit, `value == line` push (whole-number line), else miss; `under`: `value < line` hit, equal push, else miss; `atleast`: `value >= line` hit else miss (no push); `yes`: hit when a qualifying touchdown is recorded, else miss. A missing `StatState` at final is `None` (`parlay_grade` leaves the leg pending and counts `stat_missing`; never a miss by absence). Period props are out of release one (`period = 'game'` only; D3). A leg DraftKings voids (a player who did not play) is recorded by the owner through `leg_status:<seq> = void` (§5.2), which grades the leg `void`; `_settle_card` treats it as a push as today for a non-correlated card and writes **no computed return** for a `correlated` card (the owner's confirmed return is the only settlement figure there; C5).

## 5. Placement, corrections and decline (`harness/dashboard/app.py`, `harness/parlay/placement.py`)

### 5.1 `POST /api/parlay/placed`

Body `{card_id: int, confirmation_id: UUID v4 string, stake: decimal string, accepted_odds: int, leg_lines: {seq: line} | null, note: string ≤ 200 | null}`; every field is type-validated (400 on a malformed body; `sanitize_reason` is applied to `note` only). The route (LAN listener only, session required, §6) opens one transaction and:

1. Takes the **week lock** `select pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('parlay_week:' || year || ':' || week))` on the card's week, then the row lock `select ... for update` on the card. The week lock is what serializes the cap across different cards; the row lock serializes submits of one card. `mark_placed` takes the same week lock, so the CLI path is covered.
2. If a placement with `(card_id, confirmation_id)` exists → `200` with that placement (idempotent). If the `confirmation_id` exists on **another** card → `409 {"refusal": "confirmation_reused"}`.
3. Else calls `mark_placed(session, card_id, accepted_odds, stake, now, leg_lines, confirmation_id=..., note=...)`, which keeps every refusal: `CardNotPlaceable` → `409 {"refusal": "card_not_placeable", "placement": <the existing placement object when the card is already placed, else null>}` (so a retry with a new id after a lost response shows the owner what was recorded: the sheet reads `already recorded at 7:41 PM · $25 at +450`); `BudgetExceeded` → `409 {"refusal": "budget_exceeded", "recorded", "left"}`; `LineMoved` → `409 {"refusal": "line_moved", "moved": {seq: [was, now]}}`.
4. An `IntegrityError` on the unique confirmation index (two truly concurrent inserts) is caught; the route re-reads and returns the existing placement as `200`.

`mark_placed` gains `confirmation_id: str | None = None` and `note: str | None = None` (additive keywords; the CLI passes none), takes the week lock, and keys the stake row to the card's `(year, week)` (§0). The weekly cap is never relaxed by the UI (design §3.2); an out-of-budget placed ticket cannot be recorded and the sheet says so; remediation is a policy decision for the user, not a route.

### 5.2 `POST /api/parlay/correct`: the closed transition table

Body `{card_id, field, new_value, note | null}`. Every call writes one `parlay_placement_corrections(id, card_id, ts, field String(16), old_value String(32), new_value String(32), note String(200))` row and applies the effect below inside one transaction under the same week and row locks; any `(card state, field, new_value)` outside the table → `409 {"refusal": "correction_not_allowed"}`. `old_value`/`new_value` longer than 32 characters → `400`. At most 20 correction rows per card (`409 {"refusal": "corrections_capped"}`).

| Card state | `field` | `new_value` | Effect | Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `proposed` | `status` | `declined` | card `void`, `declined_reason = 'declined'`; row `(status, proposed, void)`; the builder builds one replacement (§2.4) | none |
| `placed` \| `alive` (card not cashed/busted) | `stake` | decimal | `stake_actual` set; the cap re-checked on an increase (`budget_exceeded`) | one `stake` row with the signed delta, `source = 'confirmed'`, the card's `(year, week)` |
| `placed` \| `alive` | `accepted_odds` | int | `dk_odds_actual` set, `dk_payout_actual` recomputed | none |
| `placed` \| `alive`, leg `seq` **not graded** | `leg_line:<seq>` | decimal | the leg's `threshold` set | none |
| `placed` \| `alive` \| `cashed` \| `busted` | `leg_status:<seq>` | `void` | the leg `void` (DraftKings voided it: player did not play); the card re-grades on the next `parlay_grade` pass | none |
| `placed` \| `alive` \| `cashed` (legs all hit or void) | `return` | decimal | the **confirmed** return: `parlay_ledger` `return` row `source = 'confirmed'`, amount = the value; card `cashed` | one row, the card's week |
| `placed` \| `alive` \| `cashed` \| `busted` | `status` | `void` | DraftKings voided the whole slip: `parlay_ledger` `void` row `source = 'confirmed'` (stake back) through `_pay`'s one-row guard; card `void` | one row, the card's week |

No other status value is accepted (`cashed`, `busted`, `placed`, `alive` are never writable). Corrections to `stake`, `accepted_odds` or an ungraded leg's line are refused once the card is `cashed` or `busted` (`409 {"refusal": "graded"}`); a graded leg's line is never corrected. Grading uses the corrected terms.

### 5.3 Idempotency and concurrency

Two submits with one `confirmation_id` record one placement and one ledger row; two ids on one card record one placement and one `409`; a phone and a laptop submitting **different** cards at the cap serialize on the week lock and record exactly one (`$25 + $25` recorded, the third `budget_exceeded`; a test); same-card submits serialize on the row lock. Corrections carry no confirmation id: the same field corrected twice with the same value writes two rows (an audit trail), which the surface shows as one `corrected` mark with the newest values.

### 5.4 Decline: `Not this one` (D5)

The design's decline action is the first row of §5.2's table: `POST /api/parlay/correct` with `field = "status"`, `new_value = "declined"` on a `proposed` card. It cannot be undone from the UI (no undo route exists); the declined card stays visible as a grey chip in Season (§1.4); exactly one replacement is built. No third route exists (U9 names two).

### 5.5 Request rules

Both routes require the session cookie (`SameSite=Strict`), `Content-Type: application/json`, the header `X-Requested-With: sports-ui`, and an `Origin` equal to `https://{LAN_ADDR}:{LAN_PORT}` **from settings, never from the request's `Host`**; anything else is `403`. Bodies are read with a hard cap of 4 KiB (`413` past it, regardless of `Content-Length`). A per-session write limit of 30 requests a minute (`429`), counted in a bounded structure. Errors carry a refusal code, never a stack trace. The response headers on the LAN app include `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.
