## 0. Amendments to v2 and to the roadmap text

- **0.1 (§7.1) The veto is asynchronous and post-hoc.** A signal is never held for the model. The executor's decision stands; the veto worker judges the signal afterwards from features frozen **as of the signal's `created_at`** (I3 of review A), calls both models, and records the decision for H9. Reason: an Opus call with web search takes 10-30 s and the executor's loop ceiling is 7.5 s. Promotion to enforcing stays a user gate and is out of scope. t7's header states that decisions are post-hoc, so H9 is an upper bound on what an enforcing veto could deliver, and t7 reports the decision lag distribution and the cached share.
- **0.2 (§7.1) Subject, bucket and cache.** Signals are grouped by a tumbling 30-minute bucket on `(game_id, market_type, bucket_start = created_at floored to 30 min)`. The worker claims a bucket, makes one paired call for its first signal (the trigger), and writes one `veto_decisions` row per signal in the bucket (`from_cache = false` for the trigger, `true` for the rest, each with the feature delta from the trigger). A bucket is re-called early when the game's ESPN status changes, a new weather snapshot lands for the game, or the sharp fair moves by 2 probability points or more (the §6.4 velocity threshold, imported from its home). The roadmap's `subject_id = signal_id` names the trigger.
- **0.3 (R:220, U4) The caps cover everything the key spends.** `veto_daily_usd_cap` $25 and `veto_weekly_usd_cap` $150 are totals across the primary, the shadow, the annotator and the parlay rationale. Enforcement: one gate function `reserve_spend(kind, worst_case_usd)` performs an atomic reservation under a transaction advisory lock on the day key (the cap is a sum over four kinds and two models plus a weekly cap, so a single-row conditional UPDATE cannot express it; plan ruling) before any call and releases the difference to the actual cost after it; the client runs with `max_retries = 0`; one tool round (a `pause_turn` ends the call); the worst-case constants are re-fitted from the first live day and journaled (A-C3).
- **0.4 (R:217) "default effort" is written as `high`**, pinned in the prompt hash.
- **0.5 (§8.1) Props are not fetched at build time in phase 5.** Legs come from moneyline, spread and total at DraftKings prices already in `odds_snapshots`.
- **0.6 (§8.1) "Recorded in `parlays`" is superseded** by the dashboard spec §3.9 tables.
- **0.7 (R:235, F64) The RFQ listener's key.** No new secret files. The listener runs inside `app-ws` on the key that service already mounts; F64 is the user's replacement of the two existing key files with a read-scoped pair, which narrows the recorder and the listener together (A-C2). The listener's module has no write path and holds no REST transport; `POST /communications/quotes` raises `PaperModeViolation` before signing (`harness/venues/kalshi/http.py:176`).
- **0.8 (R:241, F71) "non-200 from the subscribe"** means: a handshake status other than 101, an error frame with code 8, 9, 10, 11 or 27 in reply to the subscribe, or a permission error at any point. Each idles the listener for one hour and writes `venue_status('kalshi_rfq')`; the market channels are unaffected because the listener owns its own connection (1.6).
- **0.9 (R:207) Futures discovery.** `GET /series` has no ticker-prefix filter; discovery filters the football categories' series client-side over the prefixes `KXNFL` and `KXNCAAF`, excluding the per-game series by exact match against `harness/venues/kalshi/public.py::FOOTBALL_SERIES` (A-I9), then pages `GET /markets?series_ticker=…&status=open&limit=1000&mve_filter=exclude`.
- **0.10 (R:209) NWS hourly schema is unverified**; the first weather task records one live `forecastHourly` response through `store_raw` and pins the parser to the recorded field names before parsing code is merged.
- **0.11 (§8.2) Decline rules.** Each leg's `market_ticker` resolves to `venue_markets.game_id`; an RFQ with two legs on one `game_id` is declined `same_game`; a leg that does not resolve falls back to `event_ticker` distinctness with `unmatched_legs` recorded; legs without a `direct` sharp fair or over the disagreement threshold decline `no_fair` / `disagreement`; a single-leg RFQ declines `single_leg`; an RFQ whose dollar exposure at our bid exceeds `rfq_collateral_cap_usd` declines `collateral` (plan ruling). F72's independence test uses the same game-level distinctness; both the event-level and game-level fee branches are stored so grading can be re-run either way (A-I2). No quote is ever sent.
- **0.12 (§7.1, H6) Phase 5 records weather for the veto; H6 is not measured this phase.** H6's instrument (`news_events` timestamp triplets, spec §9.7) is not built; it is a later phase's item, listed in §6 (B-I3).
- **0.13 (§6.7) No `no_veto` secondary while the veto is shadow-only** (R2, a roadmap ruling): H9 is measured within the primary by decision label; a live `no_veto` would be a user gate and a dated amendment (B, conformance 3).
- **0.14 (§7.2) t7 and t10 are built this phase** from `veto_h9` and `rfq_quotes`, replacing the `_not_collected` placeholders (B-M10).

## 1. Components

### 1.1 Futures and ladder weekly snapshots (item a, H7)

- **Job** `futures_snapshot`: an APScheduler `CronTrigger(day_of_week='tue', hour=9, minute=0, timezone='America/Chicago')` registered in `harness/scheduler.py` (which builds its scheduler with `timezone="UTC"`, so the trigger carries its own; B-M6), running in `app-run`; plus `harness futures snapshot` for a hand run. Every pass writes a `job_runs` row (`job = 'futures'`, `notes.trigger ∈ {cron, manual}`, the reached series set, request count).
- **Discovery** per 0.9: categories from `GET /search/tags_by_categories` (recorded once per pass); series ordered deterministically by ticker; the resume point persisted in `job_state('futures.resume')` as the index into the deterministic series order (`job_state.value` is an integer), the ticker recorded in `job_runs.notes.resume_after`, and the pass restarts at zero with `resume_reset` when the two disagree (A-I8; plan ruling).
- **Storage**: raw bodies through `store_raw(source='kalshi_futures')`; normalized `futures_snapshots(id, run_id, snapshot_week (ISO text), series_ticker, event_ticker, market_ticker, title, yes_sub_title, kalshi_market_type ∈ {binary, scalar}, strike_type, floor_strike Numeric, cap_strike Numeric, yes_bid, yes_ask, last_price (Numeric(10,4), decoded from the `*_dollars` strings by the phase 4 decoder), volume Numeric(14,2), open_interest Numeric(14,2) (decoded from the `*_fp` strings), close_time, fetched_at)`; index `(series_ticker, snapshot_week)` in `_INDEX_DDL`. Retained for the season (housekeeping deletes nothing).
- **Budget**: 500 requests per pass (plan ruling 2026-09-10: the live Sports catalogue holds 458 football series after the exact exclusion, so 200 would snapshot each series only every third week), counted per request, 0.1 s between pages; beyond it the pass stops, records `budget_exhausted` and resumes next pass; a non-200 page stops the pass with `job_runs.status = 'error'` and the endpoint, status and 120 characters of body in `notes.errors`. No Odds credits.
- H7's measurement is a later report table; this phase records.

### 1.2 NWS forecast snapshots (item b; weather for the veto; H6 recorded only)

- **Stadiums**: `harness/weather/stadiums.yaml` keyed by `(sport, team_id)` with `abbreviation` for humans, `name, lat, lon, roof ∈ {open, dome, retractable}, source` (a public URL per row), written by the implementer from public sources for every `teams` row with a `popularity_tier`; `neutral_sites.yaml` keyed by `(sport, home_team_id, away_team_id, kickoff_date)`; `stadiums_missing.txt` lists the rest with a reason; a coverage test enforces the union. A game with no stadium row is skipped (reason once per game in `job_runs.notes`); a dome is skipped; a retractable roof is fetched and labelled.
- **Client**: `harness/feeds/nws.py`, its own read-only httpx client (precedent `harness/venues/kalshi/http.py:16-30`), `User-Agent: sports-harness/1 (self-hosted research harness)` verbatim from the roadmap (a 403 or blocklist on it is a **user gate**, never an edit; A-M11), `Accept: application/geo+json`, GET only, host `api.weather.gov`.
- **Flow**: `/points/{lat},{lon}` once per stadium into `weather_points(sport, team_id, office, grid_x, grid_y, forecast_hourly_url, fetched_at)`; a 404 or 301 on the hourly URL re-resolves `/points` once per stadium per day, a second failure records the stadium and lowers the Pulse weather-coverage count (A-I6). Then `forecastHourly` per outdoor game inside 72 h, hourly, as a recorder-tick source that runs only when the cadence in force is 300 s or 900 s and at least 25 s remain in the tick budget (A-I7, B-I10), fetch order oldest-snapshot-first (A-M12), one five-second retry on 429, any other non-200 recorded and skipped. Raw through `store_raw(source='nws')`; normalized `weather_snapshots(id, run_id, game_id, fetched_at, period_start, temperature_f, wind_mph, wind_dir, precip_pct, short_forecast (sanitized, ≤ 80 chars), roof)` for kickoff −1 h to +4 h; index `(game_id, fetched_at)` in `_INDEX_DDL`; retained for the season.
- **Schema pin** (0.10): the parser rejects a body missing a pinned field with a `job_runs.notes` entry.

### 1.3 Parlay CLI (item c)

- **Commands** `harness parlay build --sport cfb|nfl [--week W]` (a `parlay_cards` row, `status = proposed`, with `parlay_legs`), `harness parlay placed <card_id> --payout <dk_american> --stake <usd> [--leg-line <seq>=<point>]` (a `parlay_placements` row and the `parlay_ledger` stake row; it refuses when the newest DraftKings row's `point` differs from the card's for any leg without a `--leg-line` for it; A-M7), `harness parlay show`. Config `harness/parlay/parlay.yaml`: weekly budget 50, smart 25, lottery 5, lottery cards max 3, anchors `LSU`, `NO`, pool thresholds.
- **Legs**: smart card 3-4 legs from different games, anchor an LSU or Saints moneyline, spread or total; other legs from the +EV pool (signals with `direct` fair and edge over the threshold in the last 6 h) priced at the newest `odds_snapshots` row for `(game_id, market_type, outcome, book='draftkings')` no older than 30 minutes, each leg's `fetched_at` printed on the card (A-M6); an anchor without a row ends the build with exit 2 and `no anchor priced`. Lottery card 6-8 legs, correlated legs allowed and labelled per §8.1. `estimated_payout = Π decimal odds`; `true_probability = Π sharp p` with independence stated on the card.
- **Budget**: `placed` refuses when this ISO week's `parlay_ledger` stakes plus the new stake exceed $50; a proposed card older than 7 days becomes `void` with `declined_reason = expired` (B-C4); the unallocated $10 stays unspent.
- **Rationale**: a template in a fan voice unless `secrets/anthropic_api_key` exists, then one `claude-opus-5` call (effort `low`, no tools, 200 output tokens, reserved through `reserve_spend('parlay', …)`), the template on any error. Text: `sanitize_model_text(text, 600)` (`harness/research/text.py`: `harness.logging_setup.redact` first, then control characters and markup stripped, apostrophes kept, truncated; A-I5); the column is `String(600)` under F50 (B-M11).
- **Settlement**: `parlay_grade` appended after `settle` in `STAGE_MODULES` (then `rfq_grade`), hourly with the chain, idempotent, resuming next hour when the budget is gone (B-I7): each leg of a `placed` card resolves through `resolve_market`'s push rules once its game is final into `hit | miss | void`; the card becomes `cashed | busted | void` when every leg is graded; `parlay_ledger` gets the `return` row. **Leg probabilities**: the recorder tick writes `parlay_leg_probs(leg_id, ts, sharp_p, book_p)` under the condition the model's docstring states verbatim, `book_p` from the DraftKings row the leg was priced from (B-I8).

### 1.4 Shadow veto (item d, H9)

- **Worker**: `harness research-worker` in a new compose service `app-research` (same image, no ports, restart unless-stopped, mounting `secrets/anthropic_api_key` read-only and **nothing else**, no Kalshi key; A-M2), dormant without the key (`research: dormant, no key`), gated by `research_worker_enabled` (documented with the NAS-side `.env` path, as the scheduler switch is; B-M14). It also hosts the annotator (1.5). Two calls at most concurrently.
- **Queue** `veto_queue(signal_id pk, game_id, market_type, bucket_start, enqueued_at, claimed_at)`: the executor enqueues every signal that produced an intent (placed or held); the worker claims a bucket (0.2). A signal whose game is final at claim time decides `veto_error` with reason `game_final`; a cancelled intent still decides (H9 counts signals).
- **Features** (harness-computed, as of `signal.created_at`; A-I3): fair history across books over the 6 h before the signal (5-minute buckets from `fair_values` and `odds_snapshots`), venue price history (`venue_quotes` mids), disagreement, fair staleness, time to kickoff, the newest weather snapshot before the signal, the ESPN status as a fixed enum; free-text features (`short_forecast`) are sanitized and placed in the untrusted block (A-M4). Injuries: not available (D21). Every timestamp explicit (UTC and America/Chicago), including `signal_created_at`.
- **Prompt**: one frozen system prompt (`cache_control ephemeral`, the last breakpoint before the features), hash in `research_notes.prompt_hash`; defaults to `proceed`; `reduce` or `veto` require quoted evidence ids; retrieved pages and free-text features are quoted, untrusted data under a fixed instruction. Tools: `web_search`, `max_uses = 3`, `allowed_domains` unset (D22), the tool's pinned type string recorded in `research_notes.tool_calls` (B-M5); the server-side search is not a harness outbound connection (roadmap (d) authorizes it; A-M5). Output: `output_config.format` json_schema `{decision ∈ {proceed, reduce, veto}, confidence, reason ≤ 300, evidence_ids}`, `additionalProperties false`. Primary `claude-opus-5`, effort `high`, adaptive thinking; shadow `claude-sonnet-5`, identical prompt and effort, every call, recorded, never used. **First step of the task**: one recorded live call proving structured output plus `web_search` parse on the installed SDK (B-I11); the veto test asserts `cache_read_input_tokens > 0` on the second call (B-M4).
- **Decision labels**: `{proceed, reduce, veto, veto_skipped_budget, veto_error}`; "decided" = the first three (D19's rate); `veto_error` covers `pause_turn`, `refusal`, a search error block, a schema-parse failure, `game_final`. `veto_decisions.decision` holds the **primary's** decision; the shadow's lives in its `research_notes` row.
- **Cost and caps** (0.3): `research_spend(day (America/Chicago), kind, model, calls, input_tokens, output_tokens, cache_read_tokens, cache_write_tokens, searches, usd_reserved, usd)`; cost = tokens at list price (cache reads ×0.1, writes ×1.25) plus searches × $0.01, searches counted from `server_tool_use` blocks (the usage sub-field when the SDK exposes it). `reserve_spend` refuses when today's `usd + usd_reserved` plus the worst case of the pair (initially 30,000 input tokens, 2,000 output tokens, three searches per model, re-fitted after day one) would exceed $25, or the week's would exceed $150; the skipped signal decides `veto_skipped_budget` with `call_id` null (B-M1). Dormant until the next America/Chicago day or Monday. Pulse: the day's spend, reservations and dormant state (`research_budget` WATCH) and `veto_rate` WATCH at 25 % of decided signals in 24 h (thresholds in `harness/health.py`); the daily line reads `research_spend`.
- **Records**: `research_notes(call_id uuid, kind ∈ {veto, annotate, parlay, study}, subject_id text (indexed), model, effort, prompt_hash, features JSONB, snippets JSONB (≤ 6 KB, truncated at write with `truncated = true`), tool_calls JSONB, output JSONB, usage JSONB, cost_usd, latency_ms, request_id, created_at, replay bool default false, arm text null)`: two rows per call under one `call_id`. `veto_decisions(signal_id pk, call_id null, decision, confidence, from_cache, feature_delta JSONB, signal_created_at, decided_at, reason_code)`. View `veto_h9`: `veto_decisions` joined to `research_notes` on `call_id` where `kind = 'veto'`, `replay = false`, `model = 'claude-opus-5'`, decision in the decided set; t7 and the invariant read the view (B-M2).
- **Injection case**: a snippet carrying an instruction to change the decision yields `proceed` with no evidence id pointing at it and a reason free of markup and control characters (F60).

### 1.5 Weekly report annotator (item e)

- Runs in `app-research`, data-triggered: the newest `report_runs` row with `provisional = false` for the current ISO week that has no `report_annotations` row (B-C2). One `claude-opus-5` call (effort `high`, no tools, structured output `{bullets: [≤ 5 × ≤ 240 chars]}`, reserved as `kind = 'annotate'`). Input: a dedicated renderer that emits cells, headers and row indices only, every venue-sourced string replaced by its row index (B-I5). Each bullet needs at least one resolving `t<k>[row,col]` citation and every number it contains must appear in a cited cell's rendered text, else it is dropped (B-I4). Survivors go to `report_annotations(report_run_id, model, prompt_hash, bullets JSONB, cost_usd, created_at)` and render inside a fenced "model-written, unverified" block. Dormant without the key.

### 1.6 Combo RFQ listener (item f, H5)

- **Connection**: inside `app-ws`, its own WebSocket connection to the settings host (`wss://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/ws/v2`, invariant 8) with its own subscribe id sequence, its own sid, and its own reconnect using the recorder's backoff helper (`ws.py` `should_reconnect` / backoff, imported, not shared state); one subscribe frame `{"cmd": "subscribe", "params": {"channels": ["communications"]}}`; the recorder's market socket, `_sids`, `_resubscribe` and `_recover_gap` never see the channel (A-C1, A-I1, B-C3). The listener module receives parsed frames only, never the socket object; its static test asserts no `POST`, no `quotes` path, no `.send(` (A-M1). Quote-event types are logged and dropped.
- **Storage on arrival**: `rfqs(id text pk, received_at, created_ts, event_ticker, market_ticker, contracts_fp Numeric, target_cost_dollars Numeric, mve_collection_ticker, legs JSONB, raw JSONB (≤ 8 KB, truncated at write with `truncated`), status ∈ {open, deleted}, deleted_ts)`; free text is never rendered raw and never placed in a prompt (F60); the report shows a 120-character quoted excerpt of `market_ticker` only. `rfqs` and `research_notes` join the forbidden-table list for every snapshot builder (B-I9); surfaces read counts from `rfq_quotes`, `veto_decisions`, `research_spend`, `report_annotations`.
- **Quote computation (stored, never sent)** per 0.11: `fair = Π leg fair`; `yes_bid = fair − margin × legs`, `no_bid = 1 − fair − margin × legs`, margin default 0.03 per leg (`rfq_margin_per_leg`); collateral cap `rfq_collateral_cap_usd` 50; F72 both branches stored: `rfq_quotes(id, rfq_id, computed_at, legs, fair, margin_per_leg, yes_bid, no_bid, fee_branch_game, fee_branch_event, fee_subtracted, yes_bid_other_branch, no_bid_other_branch, declined_reason, unmatched_legs, graded_at, closing_fair, closing_stale bool, pnl_yes, pnl_no)`.
- **Grading**: `rfq_grade` (after `parlay_grade`) grades at the legs' settlement against the product of closing leg fair values, both branches; t10 states the P&L is a no-fill, no-adverse-selection counterfactual and an upper bound; the scored side is the RFQ creator taking our bid on the side the RFQ asked for; a quote with any stale closing leg is reported separately (B-I6).
- **Idling** (0.8): `venue_status(venue='kalshi_rfq', env='prod', status='unavailable', reason=<120 chars>)`, resubscribe after one hour; gated by `rfq_listener_enabled` (B-M3).
- **Refusal tests**: the static test above; the transport test naming `POST /communications/quotes`; a hostile `raw` reaching no rendered string.
